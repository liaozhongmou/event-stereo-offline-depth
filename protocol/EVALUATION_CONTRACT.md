# Reconstructed evaluator v1, 2026-10-20

Authority: EXECUTION_HANDOFF_ZH.md sections A2/A3. The original frozen evaluator is missing; this implementation is a reconstruction and must pass historical checks before new/old advantage comparisons.

Inputs are rectified integer x/y, seconds on the recorded epoch time base, and disparity in pixels. Prediction times may be unordered; source row order breaks equal-distance ties. Nonfinite prediction disparity is an abstention. Nonfinite times, noninteger/out-of-bounds coordinates, inconsistent lengths, or non-increasing GT times fail explicitly. No unit guessing, timestamp fitting, interpolation, coordinate repair, or error-dependent rejection.

The interval is explicit and closed. Both GT frames and prediction times must lie inside it. Each prediction maps to its nearest GT frame across the supplied GT stream; equidistant frames choose the earlier frame. Accept stored floating-point absolute time difference <= 0.015 seconds. Retain finite GT disparities in [4,28] inclusive; keep all finite predicted disparities, including values outside that range. For duplicate (frame,y,x), choose smallest time difference then earliest source row, independently of disparity/error/confidence. The denominator counts every valid GT pixel in the fixed interval, including frames without predictions.

Report native and intersected common units, coverage as a fraction, MAE/RMSE/median/P90 in px, Bad-1/2/3 as fractions using error > threshold, and correct-1/2/3 divided by the complete GT denominator. Empty support has zero coverage and null errors. Frame summaries preserve the ordered GT frames, including empty frames. Bootstrap: six-frame moving blocks, 2000 replicates, seed 20260905, mean of nonempty frame MAE in each resampled stream; a completely empty sample is excluded and counted. No significance claim is inferred from point improvements.

Development GT is explicitly the archived `*_gt_disp.npz:disp` times 0.8819476, following the manuscript's recorded conversion, with `ts` unchanged. Raw `*_gt_depth.npz` has a different time axis and is not substituted. Historical checksum/provenance gaps remain reported. Calibration/rectification is author-confirmed; no second rectification is applied.

Record exact interval and discrepancies; do not tune it to fit numbers. Failure stops advantage comparisons and C/D, while A/B diagnostics and draft editing continue.

B1 diagnostic frozen before GT scoring: score top 100/50/25/10/5%; residual bottom 100/50/25/10/5%, absolute quantiles from ALL Indoor 1 raw matcher rows (NumPy linear quantiles), reused unchanged on Indoor 2/3. First associate/deduplicate all raw predictions once. Select those fixed units by their chosen source row's score/residual; no replacement by a farther-in-time row after rejection. This measures confidence discrimination on a fixed evaluation population, rather than evaluating an implemented event-level rejection algorithm. Controls draw exactly the same number of associated units without replacement, 100 repetitions, seed 20261020; at 100% retention one deterministic full-population replicate suffices. Report empirical 2.5/50/97.5 percentiles. These control distributions are not temporal confidence intervals. All three sequences are development data. Matcher confidence precedes the row-median disparity adjustment.
